Hunt the Wumpus

How to play

  • Move around the maze hunting the wumpus.
  • If you fall in a pit, you die.
  • If you enter the wumpus's space, you die.
  • If you fire your arrow and miss, you die!
  • Spaces adjacent to the wumpus are colored red.
  • When you know the wumpus's location, switch to arrow mode and fire an arrow from an adjoining space.

Controls

  • Move up / Fire arrow up
  • Move down / Fire arrow down
  • Move left / Fire arrow left
  • Move right / Fire arrow right
  • Toggle arrow mode
